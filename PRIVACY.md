# Privacy Policy — Trail Dawn (MyBikeTracker)

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Trail Dawn is a local bicycle tracker. There is no account and no cloud sync. Ride history, bikes, and journal entries live on your device.

## Location

The app asks for **When In Use** first so it can show you on the map and record a ride while it is open. **Always** and background location are turned on only while a ride is being recorded, then turned off when the ride ends.

If you export a backup, the file includes precise GPS tracks. You choose when to share it.

## Health

If you enable Apple Health, the app **writes** cycling workouts, estimated calories, optional heart rate, and the GPS route. It does not read Health data.

## Bluetooth

Heart-rate and cadence sensors are used only after you scan or reconnect them in Settings.

## Map services

Optional [Mapbox Map Matching](https://docs.mapbox.com/api/navigation/map-matching/) can send ride coordinates over HTTPS to snap a finished track to cycling roads. This runs only when a Mapbox token is configured. Apple MapKit Directions is used for on-map cycling routes.

## Diagnostics

Product events and MetricKit crash summaries stay on the device. They are not sent to a third-party analytics service.

## Contact

Open the in-app Privacy Policy in Settings, or this file in the project repository: https://github.com/ScreaMonhik/MyBikeTracker/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
